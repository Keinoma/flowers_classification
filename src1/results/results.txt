ANALYSIS & COMMENTS
-----------------------------------------------------------

Sunflower (Best Performer):
- Achieves the highest precision (0.90) and near-perfect recall (0.98).
- The model rarely misclassifies sunflowers and misses almost none.
- Explanation: Sunflower is the only flower that is consistently yellow with very similar appearances across images (see figures, not included here). This visual consistency likely contributes to the excellent performance.

Tulip:
- Shows good precision (0.92) but a weaker recall (0.75).
- Some tulips are missed by the model. Further investigation is needed to understand why these specific cases are overlooked.

Orchid & Lotus:
- Results are acceptable but not outstanding.
- Both precision and recall scores need improvement.

Lilly (Most Problematic):
- Lowest scores: precision (0.69), recall (0.72), F1 (0.70).
- Lilly images vary greatly in shape and color (see figures, not included here), making them harder to recognize.
- Both precision and recall are low, indicating two issues:
  1. The model fails to recognize many actual Lillies.
  2. The model falsely labels other flowers as Lillies too often.
- Suggestion: Improve the model's capacity to both identify Lillies correctly and reduce false positives.

-----------------------------------------------------------
GENERAL OBSERVATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Overall precision is approximately 80% for both weighted and macro averages.
- Classes are relatively balanced, so there is no significant gap between weighted and unweighted scores, which is expected.

-----------------------------------------------------------
CONFUSION MATRIX INSIGHTS
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Tulip misclassifications:
  - When the model predicts "Tulip", it is rarely wrong.
  - However, 25% of real Tulips are missed.
  - These missed Tulips are misclassified almost equally across other classes, with no single dominant misclassification target.

- Orchid misclassifications:
  - When a real Orchid is missed, it is most often misclassified as "Lilly".
  - This specific error pattern is interesting and worth investigating further (unlike the more evenly spread errors seen with Tulips).

-----------------------------------------------------------
GRAD-CAM ANALYSIS (Overfitting Concern)
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Grad-CAM heatmaps were generated to understand which image regions the model focuses on.
- Focus was placed on Orchid images (see figures, not included here).
- The heatmaps reveal clear signs of overfitting.
- This is surprising given the relatively good overall results, but it indicates that the model may be relying on irrelevant background features rather than the flower itself.
